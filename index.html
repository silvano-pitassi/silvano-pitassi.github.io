Research Interests

My research activity focuses on the development and application of advanced numerical methods for electromagnetic field problems, with particular emphasis on the rigorous mathematical analysis of numerical simulation algorithms. More specifically, I develop polyhedral methods on general meshes, combining computational electromagnetics, functional analysis, computational topology, and scientific software.
